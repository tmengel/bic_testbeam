KCU0 waveforms: what the detector does

  • Decoding bit-exact, shape is the detector: rise samples 5-6, peak 7-8, tail 55 % at sample 19 (25 ns samples)
  • Per-event baseline shift 17-35 ADC, common to all SFIL channels of a chip (not paddles) → per-event pedestal from samples 0-3
  • Four cells per layer, stacked vertically, beam in cell 3. 20-36 % of beam-cell pulses at the ADC ceiling (1023), 2-2.5 % on an earlier particle’s tail, SFIL1 North board noisy (40-50 ADC)
