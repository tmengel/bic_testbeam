Where the triggers go: the DAQ loss budget

  • One trigger = 20 samples x 8 links on one timestamp, numbered by the board counter
  • 4.10 kHz issued on both boards, 2.8 kHz recorded (union of three streams)
  • KCU0: ~23 % lost to mid-packet UDP drops (~one receive buffer each). KCU1 silent 80 % of the time. CAEN dead-time limited at 1.0 kHz
  • 57 % of issued triggers → complete KCU0 waveform. Offline recovers ~95 % of what was received, rest is DAQ: receive buffer, common busy, dropouts, CAEN record length OPEN
