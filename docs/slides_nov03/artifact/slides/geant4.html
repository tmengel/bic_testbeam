Geant4 model of the August stack: what is in it

  • Pb / ScFi matrix as ePIC: Pb + glue (96.2 % Pb, 8.56 g/cm3, X0 1.45 cm), 1 mm fibres on a 1.34 x 1.22 mm lattice, 17 rows per SFIL (1.43 X0), 0.6 mm Pb skins
  • SFIL1-5: 4 cells x 19.05 mm, fibres 505-519 mm, surveyed x and z (pitch 63-66 mm, air between)
  • AstroPix: 9-chip module before SFIL1, 500 um sensitive Si + readout stack + carbon stave, 0.5 mm pixels
  • Baby BCAL: 22 cm Pb / ScFi, 10 depth segments x 4 columns, 58 cm fibres, Al plates, 51 mm behind SFIL5
  • Paddles at surveyed positions. Beam e+ 3-6 GeV, x = +30 mm (P6) / +80 mm (P5), y = -5 mm, 5 mm spot. Geant4 10.7, FTFP_BERT_EMZ
