Backup: why the cosmic muons are near-horizontal

  • Paddles: vertical slabs 60-180 mm upstream at beam height → a vertical muon never crosses a paddle and a cell
  • Paddle pulse on 52 % of all triggers but 97-100 % of SFIL3 candidates, all four paddles. Candidate rate 45 x higher with a paddle pulse
  • SFIL3 candidates with SFIL2 South activity in ~60 % (1 % random): tracks through SFIL1-3 in sequence. Layers side by side along the beam → a vertical muon crosses one layer only
  • Rate 1.4 / min = near-horizontal flux through 33 cm2 of paddle face into 0.6 sr of SFIL3. Paddle flat on the stack would give ~30 / min
  • Simulation: 2 M muons from a plane above → 2-12 paddle + SFIL3 crossings per cell. Every muon through a paddle → 0.16 % of 10 M
