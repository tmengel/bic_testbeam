sim/analysis/photostat_ns.py, stack_shares_calib.py, sat_gain.py. The three methods agree on the relative gains. Only the absolute scale needs the assumed energy (step 6) or the cosmic-muon scale. SFIL4 North and SFIL1 South gains are not measured directly (SFIL4 North anomalous, SFIL1 North masked). The saturation-fraction gain is 1.3 x the mean-amplitude gain in every layer and energy, a known feature of the two estimators, not a disagreement on the ratios.
