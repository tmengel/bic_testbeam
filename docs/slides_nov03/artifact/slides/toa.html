Chip time stamps (TOA): what they give in run 3062

  • Chip time stamp (TOA): 10-bit code within the 25 ns sample
  • KCU1 single SiPMs: works, ~190 ps per channel after self-calibration, ~200 ps per end N - S realistic with a threshold scan
  • KCU0 summed channels (SFIL2 beam cell): usable after calibration, but noise stamps on 14-37 % of samples share the pulse codes. Position along the fibre from N - S: 220-250 mm per shower (TOA) vs 120-146 mm (ADC waveform)
  • N and S times move together: trigger latched on the 25 ns clock, common event time (CAEN paddle time removes it). TOT only on pulses at the ceiling: amplitude on KCU1, nothing for timing
