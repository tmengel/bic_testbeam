Split from three widths per pulse-height bin (TOA N-S, ADC N-S, TOA minus ADC), assuming independent errors: shared 1.4-1.9 ns, TOA own 1.6-1.8 ns, ADC own 0.8-1.2 ns. The shared part limits any readout of this summed channel. The TOA error does not shrink for bigger pulses, so it is not electronic noise; a likely cause is the low threshold firing on the first few photons of the slow summed pulse (not tested).
