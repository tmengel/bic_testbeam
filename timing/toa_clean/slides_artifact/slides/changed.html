The earlier code-to-time table was the most likely ADC crossing phase per code, and only words within 6 ns of the ADC crossing were kept; both pull the TOA towards the ADC, which made the TOA narrower and compressed its time scale (226 mm/ns instead of the fibre value). The split of the spread into TOA-only, ADC-only and shared parts assumed independent errors, which those calibrations were not, so it is dropped. With independent calibrations, the TOA and ADC North - South residuals inside a paddle correlate by 0.38.
