Black: the word on the pulse sample (k or k+1). Red: every word in triggers with no pulse on this channel (amplitude below 15 ADC), samples 1-18; there the rate is 8 percent (South) and 26 percent (North) per sample. A noise word on the pulse sample is taken as the pulse word, which gives the tails on the next slides. The cause of the noise words is open. The earlier analysis kept only words within 6 ns of the ADC crossing, which does not remove them either: a random word lands inside a 12 ns window about half the time.
