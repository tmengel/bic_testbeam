TOA time without the ADC

t = 25 ns × (sample of the word) + T(code)

  • The ADC only finds the pulse and its 25 ns sample; the TOA word on that sample, else the next
  • T(code): the code distribution spread over 0–25 ns (the beam is not locked to the 40 MHz clock)
  • No time window, no tuning against the ADC; built on even triggers, every result on odd triggers
